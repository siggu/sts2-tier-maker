{{C2|Crimson Mantle}} is a 1 {{Icon2|IE}} cost {{QueryLink2|Cards|rarity:Rare&color:Ironclad|Rare}} {{QueryLink2|Cards|type:Power&color:Ironclad|Power}} Card for the {{KW2|Ironclad}}.
== Interactions ==
=== Synergies ===
==== Cards ====
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss triggers {{C2|Rupture}}, giving the user 1 (2 if upgraded) {{BD2|Strength}}.
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss triggers {{C2|Inferno}}, dealing 6 (9 if upgraded) damage to ALL enemies.
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss can activate {{C2|Spite|Spite's}} additional hit(s).
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss contributes toward {{C2|Tear Asunder|Tear Asunder's}} hit count.
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle}} gaining {{KW2|Block}} triggers {{C2|Juggernaut}}, dealing 6 (8 if upgraded) damage to a random enemy.
* {{C2|Panic Button}} does not prevent {{KW2|Block}} gain from {{BD2|Crimson Mantle}}.
==== Relics ====
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle}}'s HP loss triggers {{R2|Centennial Puzzle}}.
* {{C2|Crimson Mantle}}'s HP loss triggers {{R2|Self-Forming Clay}}.
* {{R2|Demon Tongue}} can reverse {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss, provided it is the first instance of HP loss in a given turn.
** Because {{R2|Demon Tongue}} ''heals'' HP rather than prevents HP loss, it does ''not'' negate any of the beneficial triggers listed under [[Slay the Spire 2:Cards List|Card]] and [[Slay the Spire 2:Relics|Relic]] synergies.
* Playing {{C2|Crimson Mantle}} with {{R2|Mummified Hand}} in play will reduce a random card's cost to 0.
* Playing {{C2|Crimson Mantle}} with {{R2|Game Piece}} in play will draw a card.
* Playing {{C2|Crimson Mantle}} with {{R2|Lost Wisp}} deals 8 damage to ALL enemies.
* Playing {{C2|Crimson Mantle}} with {{R2|Permafrost}} gains 7 {{KW2|Block}} if it's the first Power played this combat.
=== Neutral ===
* {{R2|Tungsten Rod}} prevents {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss, but this in turn negates all HP loss synergies.
* {{P|Lucky Tonic|Lucky Tonic's|2}} {{BD2|Buffer}} effect negates {{C2|Crimson Mantle|Crimson Mantle's}} HP loss.
** With this in mind, {{C2|Crimson Mantle}}'s damage trigger can inadvertently delete an instance of {{BD2|Buffer}}, which could otherwise block a much larger amount of damage.
=== Anti-Synergies ===
* Obtaining {{R2|Spiked Gauntlets}} from {{KW2|Tanx}} raises the cost of this card by 1.
